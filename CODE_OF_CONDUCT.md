# Code of Conduct

PromptSift should be a welcoming, professional, and technically rigorous project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, direct, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while still addressing concrete risks.
- Welcome questions and contributors with different experience levels.
- Respect privacy; do not publish credentials, private code, or personal information.
- Give credit for ideas and work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, doxxing, deliberate exposure of secrets, and personal attacks are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates these expectations. Responses may include a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban, depending on severity and history.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and project-related public interactions.
